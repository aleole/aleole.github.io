##  <u> Open positions </u>

### Postdoctoral Position in Multimessenger Astrophysics and Stellar Evolution Near Massive Black Holes 
Application deadline: November 15, 2026.

I am looking for a postdoctoral researcher to join us in Warsaw and work on **stellar IMRI/EMRI-type systems**.
The project is broad in scope, and candidates with a range of backgrounds and expertise are encouraged to apply, including in **stellar and binary evolution, hydrodynamical simulations of stellar interactions, stellar oscillations and tidal interactions, modelling or observations of electromagnetic counterparts and tidal disruption events, and gravitational-wave astrophysics**.

More details about the position, application procedure can be found in the official announcement on the Institute website below. I am also very happy to answer any questions about the position or the project:

**[Full job announcement](https://camk.edu.pl/en/archiwum/2026/09/09/postdoctoral-position-in-multimessenger-astrophysics-and-stellar-evolution-near-massive-black-holes/)**
